Our Mission: Building Laborers

The mission of Campus Outreach is to **Glorify God by Building Laborers on the Campus for the Lost World.** This statement guides everything we do, from our weekly large groups to one-on-one discipleship meetings.

Four Foundational Commitments:

  • **Evangelism:** Clearly proclaiming the gospel message to the lost.
  • **Discipleship:** Training believers to follow Christ and lead others.
  • **Indigenous Leadership:** Developing student leaders who can sustain the ministry.
  • **World Missions:** Sending laborers out from campus to the world.
